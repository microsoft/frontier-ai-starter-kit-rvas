# Lesson 3 — Run approval and select delivery options

## Goal

Make an accountable decision to approve, revise, or stop a proposed publication.

## Duration

20 minutes.

## Audience

SME, legal/compliance, brand/communications, content owner, accessibility lead, procurement/security representative, and facilitator.

## Prep

Bring the Lesson 2 artifact and use `../accelerator/sample-data/approvals.json` as a non-production approval-record example. Prepare current official documentation for any platform candidates; do not assume a service capability.

## Timed activity

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6 min | SME checks each line against its linked source and records approve, revise, or reject. |
| 6–11 min | Legal/compliance checks obligations, privacy, consent, disclosure, retention, and prohibited use. |
| 11–15 min | Brand and accessibility review presentation, transparency, captions, transcript, fallback, and language behavior. |
| 15–18 min | Content owner accepts lifecycle responsibility, review date, and pause/withdraw authority. |
| 18–20 min | Compare delivery candidates for rights, accessibility, operations, audit, cost, and exit path. |

## Artifact

A human approval record and vendor-neutral delivery capability decision.

## Expected output

The record names approvers, roles, decisions, timestamps, publication conditions, and a rejection/revision path. The selection decision documents evidence rather than a presumed vendor API.

## Validation

No `approved-for-demo-only` or production-equivalent status is valid without approved SME, legal/compliance, brand/communications, and content-owner decisions. The mock renderer enforces this rule for its fixture.

## Debrief

Who can pause or withdraw a published experience when a source changes, a defect appears, or consent is withdrawn?

## Next decision

Choose the pilot channel and proceed only with the approved revision and documented accessibility alternatives.
