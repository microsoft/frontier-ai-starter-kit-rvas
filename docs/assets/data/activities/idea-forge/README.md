## When to use Idea Forge

Use Idea Forge at the start of an engagement when you know the customer or industry, but not the first useful AI proof. It is an intake tool: it helps the team turn public research and customer signals into ranked, right-sized ideas before choosing a scenario playbook.

It is not an architecture approval. The scenario conversation still validates source ownership, access, environment, operating model, and evidence for the next decision.

## Five-minute idea funnel

1. Prepare the customer or business-unit name, industry, and any known region, segment, or pain points you are allowed to use.
2. Run Customer Activity-Forge for public-source research and approximately ten researched, right-sized ideas.
3. Pick the idea with a narrow user, a safe representative sample, an accountable owner, and a believable first evidence case.
4. Choose the scenario playbook that best matches the customer outcome: AI Grounding / IQ, Content Understanding and Document Workflow, or Avatar Scenario.
5. Select the scenario lessons needed for the next customer decision.

The goal is not to commit to an architecture during intake. The goal is to arrive at a transparent
working direction, the assumptions to verify, and a useful first demonstrator.

## What to prepare

**Required**

- Customer name or business unit.
- Industry or sector.

**Helpful if known**

- Region or customer segment.
- Allowed pain points or public signals to verify.
- Any data, content, or process boundary already discussed.

**Keep out**

- Confidential account-plan or CRM details.
- Unapproved customer data.
- Claims the team cannot cite or validate.

## What Idea Forge produces

- **Research summary:** three to five sentences with public-source citations and explicit coverage gaps.
- **Ranked ideas:** approximately ten ideas ranked by customer fit, achievable first proof, and differentiation.
- **Scenario mapping:** each idea names a primary scenario direction and the first customer decision to take forward.
- **Scenario handoff:** the top idea gets a pre-filled handoff with assumptions clearly marked for customer confirmation.

## Sample handoff into a scenario

This example shows the shape of the handoff. Replace the placeholders with the skill output and keep anything unconfirmed visible for the customer conversation.

| Scenario input | Pre-filled direction |
|---|---|
| Customer outcome | Reduce time spent finding trusted answers for a specific user group. |
| Target users and access boundary | Named role and access boundary to confirm with the customer. |
| Context and source owner | Approved corpus, data product, or synthetic representative sample plus the accountable owner. |
| Recommended scenario | AI Grounding / IQ, Content Understanding and Document Workflow, or Avatar Scenario. |
| Golden-dataset / evidence starter | Routine case, edge case, refusal/review case, or access case that proves the first outcome. |
| First customer decision | The next decision the scenario playbook should help the customer make. |

## Next step

Once the top idea has an owner, a safe sample, and a first evidence case, move it into the scenario page. Open the GitHub skill only when you are ready to run the research prompt.
