# Lesson 4 — Pilot, observe, and improve

## Goal

Confirm that the approved experience works for employees and can be operated safely.

## Duration

25 minutes.

## Audience

Pilot owner, content owner, support lead, accessibility lead, analytics/privacy representative, and facilitator.

## Prep

Bring the approved revision, withdrawal contact, pilot cohort definition, and the aggregate-only `../accelerator/sample-data/feedback-fixture.json` example. Do not collect employee identifiers unless an approved need requires them.

## Timed activity

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 min | Define a limited cohort, channel, locale, help path, review date, and the person who can pause publication. |
| 5–12 min | Test disclosure, captions, transcript, keyboard/mobile navigation, language choices, fallback, and withdrawal route. |
| 12–18 min | Select minimized measures by published revision: completion, drop-off, accessibility use, task success, questions, support handoffs, feedback, and defects. |
| 18–22 min | Review synthetic evidence and identify one experience improvement and one operational risk. |
| 22–25 min | Decide whether to iterate, scale, pause, or withdraw; route any new claim through Lessons 1–3. |

## Artifact

A pilot evidence review and an explicit iterate, scale, pause, or withdraw decision.

## Expected output

Measures are aggregated where possible, tied to a revision and cohort, and accompanied by an owner and review date. The team can identify the approved artifact to withdraw.

## Validation

Verify that the published revision has the recorded approvals, disclosure, transcript, fallback, human help route, and withdrawal owner. A source correction or a new claim restarts the grounding and approval flow.

## Debrief

What evidence would make the team pause the pilot even if completion is high?

## Next decision

Set the next content review date and either authorize a controlled scale-up or return the artifact to the appropriate earlier lesson.
