# Lesson 5 — Operating evidence

## Goal

Define the evidence, review triggers, and accountable owners that keep grounding trustworthy after the first demonstration.

## Duration

35 minutes.

## Audience

Service owner, source owner, evaluator, security/privacy reviewer, operations lead, and facilitator.

## Preparation

Bring the pattern decision and golden dataset. Identify the customer’s approved evidence location and incident process; do not introduce a new data store in this lesson.

## Timed activity

| Time | Facilitation step |
|---|---|
| 0–10 min | Name owners for source lifecycle, access decisions, service operation, evaluation, and incidents. |
| 10–20 min | List change events: source content, schema, permission, context/model, product capability, and incident. |
| 20–30 min | For each event, choose evidence, approver, golden-set rerun requirement, and go/no-go decision. |
| 30–35 min | Rehearse an incident: an answer cited a stale source; decide containment, review, and remediation evidence. |

## Artifact

An operating-evidence plan with event, owner, retained evidence, review cadence, approval authority, and escalation route.

## Expected output

The team records source owner and version, access-test results, golden-set results, incident decision, and remediation. A source or permission change triggers impact review and appropriate golden-set rerun.

## Validation

Each change event has one accountable owner, a retained evidence location, and a clear condition for pause, rollback, or expansion.

## Debrief

Ask: “Who can decide that yesterday’s evaluation no longer applies?” If the answer is unclear, ownership is incomplete.

## Next decision

Approve a limited pilot only when the boundary, pattern, golden set, and operating-evidence plan have named owners and acceptance criteria.
