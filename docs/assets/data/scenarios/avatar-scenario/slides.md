# Avatar Scenario
## Customer workshop · v0.1.0

A governed avatar-led experience, not merely a generated video.

---

# Why this scenario

- Make approved content clearer and easier to revisit
- Serve languages, channels, and accessibility needs
- Preserve accountable human ownership
- Learn from employee feedback and operating evidence

**Success:** useful employee experience + traceable published content.

---

# The end-to-end path

1. Approved onboarding content
2. Script and storyboard
3. SME, legal, and brand approval
4. Avatar, voice, and channel choice
5. Employee experience
6. Feedback and operational evidence

A change to a claim returns to approval.

---

# Start with approved content

For each claim, capture:

- source ID, version, owner, and review date
- intended audience and locale
- policy sensitivity and help/escalation path

Do not let retrieval or generation become an unreviewed source of policy.

---

# Make the script auditable

| Script segment | Grounding link | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Benefits enrolment | `ONB-001@version#benefits` | SME review |
| Safety expectation | `ONB-002@version#safety` | Legal review |

Storyboard the message, visuals, captions, transcript, and fallback together.

---

# Human approval is a gate

**Required before publishing:**

- SME: factual accuracy
- Legal/compliance: obligations, claims, privacy
- Brand/communications: identity and tone
- Content owner: lifecycle and withdrawal authority

No approval record, no production render or release.

---

# Choose platform deliberately

Assess, do not assume:

- consent, likeness and voice rights
- privacy, residency, retention, and audit export
- accessibility and language coverage
- identity, channel, cost, support, and exit path

The scenario works with a selected vendor, a mock adapter, or a BYO platform.

---

# Design an honest employee experience

- State that the presenter is an avatar or AI-assisted experience
- Never impersonate a person or conceal synthetic media
- Provide captions, transcript, keyboard/mobile access, languages, and a non-avatar option
- Offer a human help route for policy questions and exceptions

---

# Capture useful evidence

Measure by published revision and cohort:

- completion and drop-off
- caption/transcript/language usage
- comprehension or task success
- questions, support handoffs, defects, and feedback

Use data minimization; aggregate where possible.

---

# Pilot decision

Choose one topic, cohort, locale, and channel.

**Exit criteria**

- approved source-to-script links
- recorded human approvals
- disclosure and accessibility checks pass
- operational owner can pause or withdraw
- evidence review date is booked
