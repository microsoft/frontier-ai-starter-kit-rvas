The experience

How it works.

Use the Decision Explorer with the customer, then take the scenario’s decisions, slides, and minimum demonstrator into the next conversation.

01
Forge or frame the need

Use Idea Forge when the customer opportunity is unclear; otherwise begin with the customer’s desired outcome and constraints.

02
Make the next decision

Use the Decision Explorer to surface the data, platform, environment, ownership, and evidence questions that shape the engagement.

03
Run the scenario playbook

Choose AI Grounding / IQ, Content Understanding, or Avatar-enabled Onboarding. Reuse lessons where they fit; do not follow a fixed course.

04
Leave an evidence trail

Capture the golden dataset, evaluation, operating assumptions, and ownership needed for the next customer decision.
