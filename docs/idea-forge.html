Intake route

When to use Idea Forge

Use Idea Forge at the start of an engagement when you know the customer or industry, but not the first useful AI proof. It is an intake tool: it helps the team turn public research and customer signals into ranked, right-sized ideas before choosing a scenario playbook.

Not an architecture approval. The scenario conversation still validates source ownership, access, environment, operating model, and evidence for the next decision.

Five-minute idea funnel

1

Prepare the prompt

Bring the customer or business-unit name, industry, and any known region, segment, or pain points you are allowed to use.

2

Run the skill

Customer Activity-Forge researches public sources only, marks gaps instead of guessing, and proposes approximately ten achievable ideas.

3

Select one direction

Prefer an idea with a narrow user, an owner, a safe representative sample, and a first evidence case the customer can review.

4

Hand into a scenario

Use the generated handoff to start the matching playbook: AI Grounding / IQ, Content Understanding and Document Workflow, or Avatar Scenario.

What to prepare

Required

  • Customer name or business unit.
  • Industry or sector.

Helpful if known

  • Region or customer segment.
  • Allowed pain points or public signals to verify.
  • Any data, content, or process boundary already discussed.

Keep out

  • Confidential account-plan or CRM details.
  • Unapproved customer data.
  • Claims the team cannot cite or validate.

What Idea Forge produces

Research summary

Three to five sentences with public-source citations and explicit coverage gaps.

Ranked ideas

Approximately ten ideas ranked by customer fit, achievable first proof, and differentiation.

Scenario mapping

Each idea names a primary scenario direction and the first customer decision to take forward.

Scenario handoff

The top idea gets a pre-filled handoff with assumptions clearly marked for customer confirmation.

Sample handoff into a scenario

This example shows the shape of the handoff. Replace the placeholders with the skill output and keep anything unconfirmed visible for the customer conversation.

Customer outcome
Reduce time spent finding trusted answers for a specific user group.
Target users and boundary
Named role and access boundary to confirm with the customer.
Context and source owner
Approved corpus, data product, or synthetic representative sample plus the accountable owner.
Recommended scenario
AI Grounding / IQ, Content Understanding and Document Workflow, or Avatar Scenario.
Evidence starter
Routine case, edge case, refusal/review case, or access case that proves the first outcome.
First customer decision
The next decision the scenario playbook should help the customer make.
